# Delete your Jigu account and data

**App:** Jigu (`com.sanjaya.jigu`)  
**Developer:** Sanjaya Rajbhandari  
**Contact:** [sanjaynewar007@gmail.com](mailto:sanjaynewar007@gmail.com?subject=Jigu%20account%20deletion%20request)

To request deletion from inside Jigu, open **Settings > Delete account > Request account deletion**. This opens an email with your account address. You can also send an email directly to **sanjaynewar007@gmail.com** with the subject **Jigu account deletion request** from the email address used for your Jigu account. Do not include your password or invitation code.

The developer will verify that the account is yours and respond with next steps. Once your request is verified and processed, your Jigu account and associated personal information, including your private mood and journal entries, device notification tokens, and content tied to your account, will be deleted. Shared content may affect your partner's space; the developer will explain any relevant shared-data handling during the request. Jigu cannot delete a photo that another person saved outside the app to their own phone.

Logging out and leaving a shared space do not delete an account. To ask for deletion of a specific shared photo or other content, describe it in your request.

See the [Privacy Policy](PRIVACY_POLICY.md) for information about data collection and retention.
