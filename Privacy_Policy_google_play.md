Jigu is a relationship app for two people. It lets you share moments, view a partner's voluntarily shared place, plan dates, celebrate time together, and keep personal mood and journal entries. This policy explains the information Jigu processes, why it is used, who can see it, and how you can control or delete it.

## Information we process

### Account and shared-space information

When you create an account, Jigu processes your email address and authentication information through Supabase Auth. Passwords are handled by Supabase Auth; Jigu does not display your password to your partner. Jigu also stores a randomly generated six-digit pairing code, your shared-space membership, and information needed to reconnect to a space you have left.

### Photos and relationship content

When you choose to share a moment, Jigu processes the photo you select from your gallery or capture with your camera, any text you add to the image, its upload time, likes, and related shared-space information. Jigu also processes the relationship start date, date invitations, invitation messages, dress style, selected venues, saved or favourite places, and a date selfie if you choose to add one.

Photos and other content in a shared space are available to the person paired with you. A partner may save a shared photo to their own phone.

### Location information

Location sharing is optional. If you enable it, Jigu processes your phone's location coordinates, the time of the update, and available accuracy and movement information while you use the app. Your paired partner can see your latest shared place in Jigu and its location widget. Jigu may show an estimated movement speed when the phone supplies usable location updates.

If you choose nearby date ideas, Jigu may use your current location to find your city and relevant places nearby. You can also choose or enter a place manually. When you open a place in Google Maps, its coordinates or address are passed to Google Maps.

Jigu does not require you to share your live location to use every feature. You can turn location sharing off in Jigu Settings or change location permission in Android Settings.

### Personal mood and journal entries

Jigu stores moods and journal entries you choose to create, along with their dates and update times. These entries are intended to be accessible only from your account through the app and are not shown to your partner.

### Notifications, device information, and local storage

To deliver moment, date, and anniversary notifications, Jigu may store a device push token and notification delivery information. Jigu stores a signed-in session and caches photos, widget images, and recent app information on your device so content can load quickly. The app and its service providers may receive ordinary connection information, such as an IP address, when your device contacts them.

## How we use information

We process information to:

- create and protect your account;
- verify email addresses and send password-reset messages;
- pair two accounts using a six-digit code;
- sync moments, likes, dates, places, and shared-space content;
- show a partner's latest voluntarily shared location;
- suggest nearby date ideas when requested;
- deliver invitations and reminders;
- maintain your private mood and journal entries;
- display content in Jigu's home-screen widgets;
- operate, secure, troubleshoot, and improve the app.

Jigu does not sell personal information and does not use it for targeted advertising.

## Who receives information

Your paired partner can see content that belongs to your shared space, including shared moments, date plans, and your location when you have enabled location sharing. Your private mood and journal entries are not shared with your partner through Jigu.

Jigu uses the following services to operate its features:

- **Supabase:** account authentication, database records, private photo storage, and backend functions.
- **Firebase Cloud Messaging:** delivery of push notifications to your device.
- **Geoapify:** nearby date-place discovery when you use that feature. A location used for discovery may be sent through Jigu's backend.
- **OpenStreetMap map-tile providers:** map images displayed in the app or widget. Map requests can include ordinary network information.
- **Google Maps:** receives a place's coordinates or address only when you choose to open that place in Google Maps.
- **Jigu's configured email delivery provider:** delivery of account confirmation and password-reset emails through Supabase Auth.

These providers process information needed for their respective services under their own terms and privacy policies. Jigu does not claim that shared-space content is end-to-end encrypted.

## Permissions and your choices

Jigu may request:

- **Location permission** to share your place with your partner or find nearby date ideas. Sharing can be turned off in Jigu Settings.
- **Notification permission** to show moment, date, and anniversary alerts. You can change this in Android Settings.
- **Camera or photo access** when you choose to capture, select, edit, share, or save an image. Jigu uses the image for the action you request.

You can leave a shared space without logging out. Leaving stops your location sharing and removes your latest shared location, but it does not delete the space's history. You may rejoin with your partner's code. Logging out ends your session on that device and clears Jigu's local shared caches. Leaving, logging out, and deleting your account are different actions.

## Retention

A shared moment normally expires after **48 hours**. If a moment is liked, it may remain in the in-app gallery beyond that period. A person may also save a copy to their phone gallery; Jigu cannot remove copies stored outside the app.

Shared dates, places, and associated images remain until they are deleted or their shared space is removed. Mood and journal entries remain until you edit or delete them or delete your account. Recent photos, session information, and widget content may be cached on your device to support the app's features.

## Account and data deletion

You can delete your account in **Jigu → Settings → Delete account**. The app asks for your current password and a typed confirmation before deletion. Account deletion removes your account, private mood and journal entries, latest location, notification devices, and every shared space you belong to. The moments, dates, saved places, and stored photos in those spaces are removed for both people, and your partner is unpaired.

If you cannot access the app, follow the instructions at https://sanjaynewar7.github.io/Jigu-/delete-account.html or email **sanjaynewar007@gmail.com** from your account email with the subject **Jigu account deletion request**. Do not send your password or pairing code by email. We will verify ownership before processing an email request.

Deleting an account cannot remove photos that either person has already saved to a phone gallery outside Jigu. For a request to remove particular shared content, contact us at the email address above.

## Security

Jigu uses authenticated access, private storage, and database access rules designed to limit access to account and shared-space data. Data is transmitted using the security mechanisms provided by the app and its service providers. No internet service can guarantee absolute security. Protect your password and do not share your six-digit pairing code publicly.

## Children

Jigu is intended for adults. It is not designed for children under 13. If you believe a child has provided information through Jigu, contact us so we can review and remove it.
